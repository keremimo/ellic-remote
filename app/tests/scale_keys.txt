# The keys through the selected track's QNT (Melodee FM-1's seq.c kb_map), done on the remote: Melodee sends the key
# (MEL_SCALE: QNT, root, scale, transpose); WHITE: C4 plays ROOT, the white keys walk the scale, the black keys are
# silent; SNAP: every key down to the scale; ALL: every key the next degree; OFF: as they are, transposed. Key 7 is C4.
